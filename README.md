# Area Code Atlas

A map game for learning telephone area codes region by region, around the world.

**Play:** https://clownien.github.io/USA-area-code-atlas/

- **Find the region:** an area code appears; click the part of the map where it rings.
- **Pick the code:** a region lights up; choose its area code.
- **Explore:** click any region to study its codes.
- **Capitals quiz:** on the world map, find the country whose capital uses an area code, or pick the code for a capital.
- **Search:** look up any area code, city, state or county in every country.

The game opens on a world map: click a country to play it, or pick one from the menu. Playable countries: USA, Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean islands (incl. Guadeloupe and Martinique), Cuba, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Ecuador, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Sweden, China, India, Japan, South Africa, Australia, Poland, the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Portugal, Ireland, Finland, Russia, Turkey, Indonesia, South Korea, New Zealand, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Croatia, Serbia, Ukraine, Slovakia, Lithuania, Belarus, Israel, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Jordan, Egypt, Iran, Morocco, Kenya, Nigeria, the Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Kazakhstan, Slovenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, North Macedonia, Albania, Montenegro, Kosovo, Moldova, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Lebanon, Syria, Iraq, Yemen, Afghanistan, Nepal, Myanmar, Cambodia, Laos, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Algeria, Libya, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Namibia, Mozambique, Ghana and Palestine. Every other country on the map shows its calling code. Countries without area codes (Central America, Haiti, Guyana, Suriname and others) explain why when you click them. Scroll, pinch or use + and − to zoom, drag to move, or jump to a part of the country from the menu. After you answer, point at any region to see its area codes.

Region borders are approximate: they are drawn around the main cities that use each code. Some of the newest codes are not included, and several countries include the main cities and towns only.

Map data: [us-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas) (U.S. Census Bureau) [@geo-insight/data](https://github.com/ibare/geo-insight) (MIT) and [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) (Natural Earth).
